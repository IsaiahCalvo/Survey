Loading feedback — five options

Imagine you just clicked Send invite. Here's what the button could look like while it's working.

Option 1 — Label changes + tiny ring
"Send invite" becomes "Sending invite…" with a small spinning ring on the left. Informative — you know exactly what's happening.
Option 2 — Centered ring only
Label disappears, a single spinning ring takes its place. Cleaner, no wording, but you don't see what action is in flight.
Option 3 — Three pulsing dots
Label disappears, three dots pulse in sequence. Calmer and more modern than a spinning ring — feels less "computer thinking", more "in progress".
Option 4 — Gold underline progress bar
Label stays exactly as is, but a thin brighter-gold bar slides left to right along the bottom edge of the button. The button looks normal — the motion just lives at the edge.
Option 5 — Soft shimmer sweep
Label dims to about half opacity and a soft light sweeps diagonally across the button on a loop. Very minimal — feels closer to a skeleton-loading shimmer than a spinner.
