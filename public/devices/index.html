One project on every screen

Survey runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install. Sign in on any device and your drawings and markups are there.

At your desk

Use Chrome, Edge or Safari on Mac or Windows. A mouse or trackpad draws, the scroll wheel zooms, and keyboard shortcuts switch tools.

On an iPad or Android tablet

Apple Pencil or a stylus draws, and your fingers pan and zoom, so you never mark a sheet by mistake. Add Survey to your home screen to open it like an app.

On your phone

Check a sheet, drop a count marker or read a note on site. The tools fit a small screen.

Are there apps to download?

Not yet. iPhone, iPad and Android apps, and Mac and Windows apps, are on the way. Until then the browser version does everything.
