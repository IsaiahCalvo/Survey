Who we are

Survey is run by Isaiah Calvo, an individual based in [state], United States (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Survey website, the Mac and Windows apps, and the iPhone and Android apps. Contact details are at the end.

What we collect

When you create an account we collect your email address. If you sign in with Google or with your company’s single sign-on, we also get the name, email and company details that service shares with us. When you use Survey we store the documents you upload; the annotations, survey data, comments and photos or recordings you add; who you invite and their role; the settings you choose; and basic technical logs needed to run and secure the service. If someone invites you or mentions you in a comment, we get your email address from them.

We use only the cookies and browser storage needed to keep you signed in and remember your settings. On phones, Survey asks for the microphone only when you use voice input, and reaches your files or share sheet only when you import or export. The desktop apps check for and install updates.

Who can see your documents

A project’s documents, marks and comments are visible to the people who have access to that project, in the role they were given (owner, contributor or viewer). Project members can see each other’s names and email addresses. We look at your content only to keep the service running and secure, when you ask us for help, or when the law requires it.

Service providers we use

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. We do share it with the companies that run parts of Survey for us, and only as much as each one needs:

Supabase (database, file storage and server code; encrypted in transit and at rest), Cloudflare R2 (a second copy of stored PDFs) [confirm: on in production], Vercel (website hosting), Stripe (payments), Brevo (sign-in, invite and notification emails), Groq and OpenAI (the AI assistant, see below), and Builder.io (usage statistics, see below). Our providers process data in [countries to confirm, at least the United States].

AI assistant

Survey’s assistant uses AI. When you type or speak a request to it, we send your request, any voice recording, and the survey data needed to answer it to Groq, which runs the AI model. If Groq cannot turn your speech into text, we may send the recording to OpenAI to do so. On phones, voice input may also use your device’s own speech recognition from Apple or Google. AI answers can be wrong; check them before you rely on them.

You can also connect an outside AI tool to your account. That tool can then read and change what your account can reach, and the tool’s own privacy policy covers what it does with that data. You can disconnect it at any time.

Helping improve Survey (optional, off by default)

In Settings → Privacy you can turn on “Let Survey learn from my survey work.” Only if you do, we may use the shapes you mark on your plans, the corrections you make to tools like Smart fill, and [confirm: the small part of the plan around each mark] to improve those tools for everyone who uses Survey. We do not use your whole documents, and we never show your marks or plans to other customers or sell them. What other customers get is a better tool, not your data. We never use data that comes from Google or Microsoft through a connected sheet or file for this. Only turn it on for work you have the right to share this way; if a project belongs to your employer or a client, ask them first.

You can turn it off at any time, and we stop collecting new work from that moment. To have the examples you already gave deleted, contact us; we remove them from our stored examples and leave them out of future training. A tool that was already improved with them keeps that improvement until it is next rebuilt.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and never touch our servers. We store your subscription status, a Stripe customer reference and [confirm: any other billing fields we keep].

Usage statistics

When you are signed in on the website or the apps, Survey sends short usage events (for example, which tools were used and whether something failed) through our own server to Agent Native Analytics (analytics.agent-native.com, run by Builder.io), which stores them for us for [period]. Each event carries a random ID for your session instead of your name or email, and our server strips email addresses, file names, links, tokens, document content and your marks before anything is sent. We use these events only to find bugs and see which features people use, never for advertising.

Connected Microsoft and Google files

If you connect Microsoft OneDrive/Excel or a Google Sheet, we read and write only the files or sheets you choose, to keep them in step with your survey. For Google Sheets, our server checks the sheet on a schedule, even when Survey is closed, until you disconnect. You can disconnect in Settings, or remove Survey’s access in your Microsoft or Google account. Copies already synced stay where they are.

Survey’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Export and deletion

You can export your data or delete your account yourself in Settings.

Deleting a document moves it to your Archive, where you can restore it for 30 days; after that it is removed from our live storage. Deleting your account removes your profile and the projects you own from our live storage [confirm: within what time]. Copies can stay in our storage backups for up to [confirmed maximum, about 5 weeks] after removal, and then expire; we do not use backups except to recover from a failure. We keep billing records as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, copy, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or limit some uses, and to withdraw any permission you gave. Most of this you can do in Settings; for the rest, email us. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

If you are in the EU or UK: we use your data to provide the service you signed up for, to meet legal duties such as tax records, for our interest in keeping Survey secure and finding bugs, and with your permission for the optional learning setting. When your data leaves the EU or UK, we rely on [safeguards to confirm]. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising, and we use no third-party advertising or cross-site tracking. Because we do not track you across other sites, we treat everyone the same whether or not their browser sends a “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signal.

Children

Survey is for work use and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.
