Privacy Policy

Survey (surveytool.app) · Effective 2026-10-20 (draft)

What we collect

When you create an account we collect your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the name and email Google shares with us. When you use Survey we store the documents you upload, the annotations and survey data you create, and basic technical logs needed to run the service.

How your documents are handled

Your PDFs and survey data are stored with our hosting provider (Supabase), encrypted in transit and at rest. Documents are visible only to you and the people you explicitly share them with. We do not send your documents to third-party processors, we do not use them for advertising, and we do not train machine-learning models on them unless you turn on the setting below.

Helping improve Survey (optional, off by default)

In Settings → Privacy you can turn on “Let Survey learn from my survey work.” Only if you do, we may use the shapes you mark on your plans and the corrections you make to tools like Smart fill to improve those tools for you and other customers. We use the marks, not your whole documents; we do not share or sell them, and we keep each company’s data separate. You can turn it off at any time, and we stop using new work from that moment. To have past contributions removed, contact us below.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and never touch our servers. We store only your subscription status and a Stripe customer reference.

Usage statistics

When you are signed in, Survey sends short usage events (for example, which tools were used and whether something failed) through our own server to Agent Native Analytics (analytics.agent-native.com, run by Builder.io), which stores them for us. Each event carries a random ID for your browser session, not your name or email, and we remove email addresses, file names, links, tokens, document content and your marks before anything is sent. We use them only to find bugs and see which features people use, never for advertising.

Optional integrations

If you connect Microsoft OneDrive/Excel, we access only the files you choose, to provide the sync features you enable. You can disconnect at any time in Settings.

Deletion

Deleting a document moves it to an archive you control, and archived items are permanently purged after 30 days. Deleting your account removes your data, subject to records we must keep for tax and accounting. Copies of your files can stay in our internal storage backup for up to about 5 weeks after that, until the backup is purged. We don't use or restore those copies, except to recover from a fault on our side.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We use no third-party advertising or cross-site tracking.
