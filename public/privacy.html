Privacy Policy

Survey (surveytool.app) · Effective November 2, 2026 · Last updated November 2, 2026

Who we are

Survey is run by Kal Voe LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company in Bergen County, New Jersey, United States (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Survey website, the Mac and Windows apps, and the iPhone and Android apps. Contact details are at the end.

What we collect

When you create an account we collect your name, your email address and the date you agreed to our Terms and this policy. If you sign in with Google or with your company’s single sign-on, we also get the name, email and company details that service shares with us. If you join a shared link as a guest, we store the name you enter.

When you use Survey we store the documents you upload; the annotations, survey data, comments, photos, videos and recordings you add (photos can include where and when they were taken if your camera saves that); who you invite and their role; the settings you choose; and the technical logs needed to run and secure the service, such as sign-in records and crash reports (crash reports have names, emails and file names removed). If someone invites you or mentions you in a comment, we get your email address from them.

We use only the cookies and browser storage needed to keep you signed in and remember your settings. On phones, Survey asks for the microphone only when you use voice input, for the camera only when you take a photo, and reaches your files or share sheet only when you import or export. The desktop apps check for and install updates.

Who can see your documents

A project’s documents, marks and comments are visible to the people who have access to that project, in the role they were given (owner, contributor or viewer). A project owner can invite others or share a link. Project members can see each other’s names and email addresses. We look at your content only to keep the service running and secure, when you ask us for help, or when the law requires it.

Service providers we use

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. We do share it with the companies that run parts of Survey for us, and only as much as each one needs:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, file storage and server code (encrypted in transit and at rest).
  • Cloudflare R2: for some accounts, a second copy of stored PDFs so they open faster.
  • Vercel: website hosting.
  • Stripe: payments.
  • Brevo: sign-in, invite, billing and notification emails.
  • Groq and OpenAI: the AI assistant (see below).
  • Agent Native Analytics, run by Builder.io: usage statistics (see below).
  • Microsoft and Google: only if you connect an Excel file or a Google Sheet (see below).

These providers process data mainly in the United States.

AI assistant

Survey’s assistant uses AI from outside companies, and Survey asks your permission before you first use it. When you type or speak a request to it, we send your request, any voice recording or photo you add, and the survey data needed to answer to Groq, which runs the AI model. If Groq cannot turn your speech into text, we may send the recording to OpenAI to do so. On phones, voice input may also use your device’s own speech recognition from Apple or Google. Under their API terms, Groq and OpenAI use this data only to answer the request and do not train their models on it; they may keep it for a short time (OpenAI up to 30 days) to prevent abuse.

We keep a history of the changes the assistant makes to your work for 90 days so you can undo them. If you send us a voice log, or hearing fails, we keep the text of what speech recognition heard and the timings for 30 days to fix problems; we do not keep the audio. AI answers can be wrong; check them before you rely on them. You can report a harmful or wrong answer with “Report answer” under it; reports are kept for 30 days.

You can also connect an outside AI tool to your account. That tool can then read and change what your account can reach, and the tool’s own privacy policy covers what it does with that data. You can disconnect it at any time.

Helping improve Survey (optional, off by default)

In Settings → Privacy you can turn on “Let Survey learn from my survey work.” Only if you do, we may use the marks you make, the corrections you make to tools like Smart fill, and the small part of the plan around each mark, from projects you own, to improve those tools for everyone who uses Survey. We do not use your whole documents, and we never show your marks or plans to other customers or sell them. What other customers get is a better tool, not your data. We never use projects you don’t own, or data that comes from Google or Microsoft through a connected sheet or file, for this. Only turn it on for work you have the right to share this way; if a project is for your employer or a client, ask them first.

You can turn it off at any time, and we stop using new work from that moment. To have the examples you already gave deleted, contact us; we remove them from our stored examples and leave them out of future training. A tool that was already improved with them keeps that improvement until it is next rebuilt.

Payments

Plans are bought on our website and processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and never touch our servers. We store your plan, its status, your trial and billing period dates, and Stripe’s customer and subscription references.

Usage statistics

When you are signed in on the website or the apps, Survey sends short usage events (for example, which tools were used and whether something failed) through our own server to Agent Native Analytics (analytics.agent-native.com, run by Builder.io), which stores them for us. Each event carries a random ID for your app session instead of your name or email, and our server removes email addresses, file names, links, tokens, document content and your marks before anything is sent. We use these events only to find bugs and see which features people use, never for advertising, and keep them only as long as they help with that. You can turn them off for a device in Settings → Privacy.

Connected Microsoft and Google files

If you connect Microsoft OneDrive/Excel or a Google Sheet, we read and write only the files or sheets you choose, to keep them in step with your survey. For Google Sheets, our server checks the sheet on a schedule, even when Survey is closed, until you disconnect. You can disconnect in Settings, or remove Survey’s access in your Microsoft or Google account. Copies already synced stay where they are.

Survey’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We never use Google or Microsoft data to train AI models.

Export and deletion

You can export your data or delete your account yourself in Settings.

Deleting a document moves it to your Archive, where you can restore it for 30 days; after that it is removed from our live storage. When you delete your account and confirm, we remove your profile and the projects you own from our live storage right away. Copies can stay in our backups for up to 7 days for the database and up to 6 weeks for stored files, and then expire; we use backups only to recover from a failure. We keep billing records as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, copy, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or limit some uses, and to withdraw any permission you gave. Most of this you can do in Settings; for the rest, email us and we will answer within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

If you are in the EU or UK: we use your data to provide the service you signed up for, to meet legal duties such as tax records, for our interest in keeping Survey secure and finding bugs, and with your permission for the AI assistant and the optional learning setting. When your data leaves the EU or UK, we rely on the safeguards our providers offer, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

What we don’t do

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising, and we use no third-party advertising or cross-site tracking. Because we do not track you across other sites, we treat everyone the same whether or not their browser sends a “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signal.

Children

Survey is for work use and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.
