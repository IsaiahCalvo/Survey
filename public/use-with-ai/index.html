Use Survey with your AI

Let an AI assistant work in Survey for you. It acts as you, with your plan and your role on each drawing. Reading your drawings works through a Claude or ChatGPT connector; marking them up happens with an agent running in your own browser.

Two ways to connect

Claude or ChatGPT — reads your drawings

Add Survey as a custom connector (steps below). It signs you in to Survey once, then finds your projects and drawings and reads the survey items on a plan. It is read-only: it does not change anything. Remove it any time at surveytool.app/agent-link.

An agent in your browser — reads and marks up

If your agent runs inside your browser while you are signed in to Survey, Survey gives it tools with no extra sign-in. It acts as you, with your role: it reads the drawing, and if you can edit it, records answers and notes and drops count markers. The drawing-markup tools (notes, shapes, highlights) stay off until you switch them on. It stops when you sign out.

Connect Claude or ChatGPT

  1. Open your AI tool's connector settings

    In Claude: Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector. ChatGPT has the same kind of setting.

  2. Paste Survey's address

    Use https://cvamwtpsuvxvjdnotbeg.supabase.co/functions/v1/survey-mcp as the server URL.

  3. Sign in to Survey

    Your AI tool sends you to Survey to sign in and press Allow. Make a free account there first if you have none. The connector now reads your drawings as you.

  4. Remove it whenever you like

    Manage and remove connectors and other signed-in agents at surveytool.app/agent-link.

Through a Claude or ChatGPT connector

Read-only.

  • See the projects and drawings you can see
  • Read the survey items on a plan

With an agent in your browser

Acts with your role; needs edit rights to write.

  • Everything the connector can read, plus the open drawing and its markup
  • If you can edit: set survey answers, add notes and drop count markers
  • Once you switch them on: add drawing markup — notes, shapes, callouts and highlights

What no agent can ever do

  • Pay or change your plan
  • Delete your account
  • Use your linked sign-ins
  • Let other agents in
  • Change your email or password

You are always in charge

An agent only ever has your rights. If you can only view a drawing, so can its agent. A browser agent needs edit rights to change anything, the drawing-markup tools stay off until you switch them on, and before anything is deleted the agent asks you to confirm; one Undo takes a change back.

To stop an agent: remove a Claude or ChatGPT connector, or any agent you let in with a sign-in, at surveytool.app/agent-link — removal takes effect at once. An agent in your own browser runs inside your signed-in session, so sign out of Survey to stop it; you can also switch its tools off for good.

Questions

Which AI tools can use Survey?

Any that speak the open connector standard (MCP). Claude and ChatGPT both let you add a custom connector, which reads your drawings. An AI running inside your own browser can also mark them up. Both act for you, with your plan and your role on each drawing.

Does the AI get its own account?

No. An agent always acts for a signed-in person and never has an account of its own. It has exactly your rights: the same role on each drawing (a viewer's agent can only read) and the same plan.

Can Claude or ChatGPT change my drawings?

No. The Claude or ChatGPT connector only reads: it finds your projects and drawings and reads the survey items on a plan. Marking a drawing up happens with an agent running in your own browser, not through the connector.

Can a browser agent change my drawings without asking?

Only if you can. A browser agent has your role, so if you can only view a drawing, so can it. If you can edit, it can record answers and notes and drop count markers, as you would. Drawing-markup tools (notes, shapes, highlights) stay off until you switch them on. Before anything is deleted the agent asks you to confirm, and one Undo takes a change back.

What can no agent ever do?

Pay, change your plan, delete your account, use your linked sign-ins, let other agents in, or change your email or password. Those stay with you.

How do I stop an agent?

A Claude or ChatGPT connector, or any agent you let in with a sign-in, you remove at surveytool.app/agent-link; removal is instant. An agent in your own browser runs inside your signed-in session, so it stops when you sign out of Survey, and you can switch its tools off entirely.

Where do my files go?

Nowhere new. The agent reads and writes the same Survey account you already use. An agent in your browser needs no extra sign-in at all.
