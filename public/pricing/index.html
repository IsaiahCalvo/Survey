Pricing questions

Is Survey free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 1 project, 5 documents and 100MB of storage with no card needed. Pro is $9.99 a month or $99 a year for unlimited projects and documents, 10GB of storage and every survey tool.

Can I cancel Pro any time?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings and keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Can my team work on the same drawings?

Yes. Invite people as viewers or editors. Enterprise adds live editing together, admin tools and single sign-on for $20 per user a month, with a minimum of 3 users.
