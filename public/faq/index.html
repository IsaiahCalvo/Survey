What is Survey?

Survey is a PDF markup app for construction and engineering drawings. You open a drawing set in your browser, mark it up with pens, shapes and count markers, and send the totals to Excel.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Survey runs in Chrome, Edge and Safari on Mac, Windows, iPad, Android tablets and phones. Open surveytool.app and sign in.

Does it work on iPad with Apple Pencil?

Yes. The Pencil draws and your fingers pan and zoom, so you never mark the page by mistake.

How do count markers work?

Pick a category, tap each item on the plan, and Survey numbers the markers and keeps a running total for every category. Export the totals to Excel when you are done.

Is Survey free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 1 project, 5 documents and 100MB of storage with no card needed. Pro is $9.99 a month or $99 a year for unlimited projects and documents, 10GB of storage and every survey tool.

Can I cancel Pro any time?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings and keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Can my team work on the same drawings?

Yes. Invite people as viewers or editors. Enterprise adds live editing together, admin tools and single sign-on for $20 per user a month, with a minimum of 3 users.

Where are my files kept?

Your markups save to the cloud so they sync to every device you sign in on. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Can Survey be set up for my company?

Yes. We build custom Excel exports, company markup templates, symbol sets and team roles. Email us with how your team works.
