Only in Survey

From the drawing set to a spreadsheet

Three things we have not found in any other PDF app.

Walk the site with the plan. Tap where each item is and answer as you go.

  • Opens in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers and LibreOffice
  • Link a Microsoft 365 workbook and edits made there come back onto the plan
  • Counts and answers from every walk in one file

Draw a region, turn the space on, and everything outside it steps back.

  • A region per page, as a rectangle or freehand
  • Export a space on its own
  • Switch spaces on and off in one tap

A template holds what you check, the questions you ask and the tags you give each item.

  • Modules, categories and a checklist for each
  • Tags like Installed, Snag or Missing
  • Shared with everyone on the project

Markup

Every markup tool, free

Pen, eraser, shapes, clouds, callouts, counts, measuring, OCR and page tools. No trial and no time limit.

Callouts, measuring, OCR and page tools are paid extras in Drawboard PDF. In Survey they are free. See the comparison

Pen

Pressure ink that follows a pen, a mouse or a finger.

Highlighter

Sits behind the linework so the drawing stays readable.

Partial eraser

Rub out part of a stroke and the rest stays.

Shapes

Rectangle, ellipse, polygon, polyline, line and arrow.

Revision cloud

Cloud a change the way you would on paper.

Count markers

Tap each item. Every category keeps a running total.

Measure

Set the page scale, then measure length and area.

Text and callouts

Notes that point at the exact spot they are about.

Text markup

Highlight, underline, squiggle and strike through words.

OCR

Read a scanned sheet on your device so you can search it.

Page tools

Insert, rotate, delete, merge and split pages.
