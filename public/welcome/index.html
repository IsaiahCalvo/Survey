What can you do with Survey?

Everything you would do with a red pen and a printed set, plus the counting you would normally do by hand.

Count markers

Tap each item on the plan and Survey numbers it and keeps the total.

Pen and highlighter

Write on drawings the way you would on paper.

Shapes, clouds and arrows

Box it, cloud it, point at it.

All features

Does it work on my iPad and phone?

Yes. The same project and the same markups follow you from your desk to the job site. On iPad, Apple Pencil draws and your fingers move the sheet.
