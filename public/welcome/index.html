From PDF to finished markup in three steps.

1. Open your PDFDrop in a drawing set from your computer or OneDrive.
2. Mark it upPen, highlighter, shapes, clouds and callouts.
3. Count and shareTap to count, then send totals to Excel or share with your team.

Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. Survey runs in Chrome, Edge and Safari. Just open surveytool.app and sign in.

Does it work on iPad with Apple Pencil?

Yes. The Pencil draws and your fingers pan and zoom, so you never mark the page by mistake.

Is it free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 1 project, 5 documents and 100MB of storage. Pro adds unlimited projects and documents, 10GB of storage, survey tools and Excel export.

Can I cancel Pro any time?

Yes. You can cancel from your account settings and keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Where are my files kept?

Your markups save to the cloud so they sync to every device. See our privacy policy for details.

Can my team work on the same drawings?

Yes. Invite people as viewers or editors. Enterprise adds live editing together, admin tools and SSO.
