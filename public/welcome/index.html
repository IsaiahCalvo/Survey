Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. Survey runs in Chrome, Edge and Safari. Open surveytool.app and sign in.

Does it work on iPad with Apple Pencil?

Yes. The Pencil draws and your fingers pan and zoom, so you never mark the page by mistake.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 1 project, 5 documents and 100MB of storage, with no card needed. Pro adds unlimited projects and documents, 10GB of storage, survey tools and Excel export.

Can I cancel Pro any time?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings and keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Where are my files kept?

Your markups save to the cloud so they sync to every device. See our privacy policy.

Can my team work on the same drawings?

Yes. Invite people as viewers or editors. Enterprise adds live editing together, admin tools and SSO.
