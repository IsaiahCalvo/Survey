Site surveys that turn into data

Walk the site with the plan. Every answer lands in a spreadsheet.

Build a checklist template once: the things you check and the questions you ask at each one. On site, drop a marker where each outlet, fitting or door is, answer Y, N or N/A, and add a note, a photo or a voice note. Export the data and you get a spreadsheet with one sheet per category plus a summary, ready for Excel, Google Sheets or anything that reads a spreadsheet. Link a Microsoft 365 workbook and edits made there come back onto the plan.

Only in Survey

Spaces and regions

Work on one part of a sheet at a time.

Name a space, pick its pages and draw a region on each one, as a rectangle or freehand. Turn the space on and everything outside the region dims. Markups made inside a space belong to it, so one sheet can carry the fit-out, the demolition and the services as separate work. Export a space on its own. We have not found this in any other PDF app.

Only in Survey

Templates and projects

Set up a survey once. Use it on every project.

A template holds the modules, the categories under each one, the checklist for each category and the tags you give each item, like Installed, Snag or Missing. Make one per kind of survey: electrical, fire, fit-out. Projects keep the drawings, the templates and the people together.
