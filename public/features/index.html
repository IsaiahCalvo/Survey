The tools you need on a drawing set

Survey gives you red-pen markup and counting in one place. Here is what each tool does.

Count markersPen and highlighterShapes, clouds and arrowsThe whole drawing set

Count markers

Tap each item on the plan and Survey numbers it and keeps the total.

Make a category for each thing you count: desks, outlets, sprinklers, doors. Every tap adds a numbered marker, and the totals for each category stay in view. Send them to Excel in one step.

Pen and highlighter

Write on drawings the way you would on paper.

Smooth ink that follows Apple Pencil, a mouse or a finger. The highlighter sits behind the linework so the drawing stays readable. The eraser takes off just what you rub out.

Shapes, clouds and arrows

Box it, cloud it, point at it.

Rectangles, ellipses, revision clouds, lines, polylines and arrows, each with its own color and line weight. Select one to move it, resize it or change its style.

The whole drawing set

Big sheets stay sharp and quick at any zoom.

Pinch or scroll to zoom from the whole sheet down to a single dimension. Thumbnails, bookmarks and search help you find the sheet you need in a large set.
