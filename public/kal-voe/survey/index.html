What Survey is for

Verify the build, device by device.

Who it’s for

Field techs, foremen and project managers who install, test and close out the job.

What you do in it

Mark up drawings, count devices, run checklist surveys on the plan, and track each install.

What comes out

Marked-up drawings, Excel reports for the office, and as-built notes that go back to Plot.

Questions about Survey

Is Survey free?

Yes. Open a PDF from your device and mark it up with no account, or sign up free for one project, five documents and every markup tool. Survey Pro is $12 a month or $120 a year.

Do I need Walkthru or Plot?

No. Survey works on any PDF drawing. In the Kal Voe Suite, it also takes drawings from Plot and sends as-built notes back.

What does the export look like?

Site surveys export as Excel (.xlsx) spreadsheets that open in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers or LibreOffice.

How much does it cost?

Survey Pro is $12 a month or $120 a year on its own, with a 7-day free trial, or included in the Kal Voe Suite for $24 per user a month.
