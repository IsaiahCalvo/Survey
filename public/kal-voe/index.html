How it fits together

From the first walk to closeout

What you capture on the walk becomes the drawing, and the drawing becomes the checklist on site.

Walkthru

Walk the site with the customer. Drop a node wherever something goes, and capture it.

  • Photos, video and notes at each node
  • The walk recorded and transcribed
  • An AI summary of what was asked for
Plot

Open the walk in Plot. Every node becomes a device you can tag, cable and schedule.

  • Devices, tags and cable runs
  • Riser diagrams and schedules
  • Title blocks and issued PDFs
Survey

Take the drawings to site in Survey. Mark them up and check off each device as it goes in.

  • Markup and counts on the drawings
  • Installed, tested, commissioned
  • Excel reports and as-built notes
