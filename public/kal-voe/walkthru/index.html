What Walkthru is for

Walk the site. Capture every detail.

Who it’s for

Sales engineers, estimators and project managers who walk a site before anything is designed.

What you do in it

Walk the space on your phone or tablet, put each request on the plan, and record what the customer says.

What comes out

A walk with every node, photo and note in place, a transcript, and a summary you can share or open in Plot.

Features

What you can do in Walkthru

01

Drop nodes on the plan

Tap the floor plan wherever a device or a question goes. Each node is numbered, so the list and the plan always match.

  • Works on phone and tablet
  • Numbered nodes on the PDF floor plan
  • Every node in one list
02

Capture everything at each node

Photos, short videos and typed notes stay pinned to the node they belong to, not lost in a camera roll.

  • Photos and video
  • Typed notes
  • All of it attached to the node
03

Record the walk, read it later

Record the conversation as you walk. Walkthru turns it into a transcript, so you can look back at exactly what was asked for.

  • Audio recording
  • Transcript of the conversation
  • Kept with the walk
04

An AI summary you can send

When you’re done, Walkthru writes up what the customer asked for. Share it with a link, or send the walk to Plot.

  • AI summary of the walk
  • Share with teammates or the customer by link
  • Send the walk to Plot

Questions about Walkthru

Do I need the other apps to use Walkthru?

No. Walkthru works on its own. In the Kal Voe Suite, a walk can also go straight to Plot.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Walkthru is made for walking a site on a phone or tablet, and you can pick the walk up on a computer later.

Can the customer see the walk?

Only if you share it. You can invite teammates or the customer with a link.

Is Walkthru free?

You can try a walk without an account. A free account keeps up to 2 active walkthroughs with 60 cloud transcription minutes a month.

How much does it cost?

Walkthru Pro is $12 a month or $120 a year on its own, with a 7-day free trial, or included in the Kal Voe Suite for $24 per user a month.
