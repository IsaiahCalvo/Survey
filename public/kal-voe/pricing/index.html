Pricing questions

Can I try the apps before I pay?

Yes. Each app can be used free with no sign-up, then with a free account. You pay only when you want Pro or the suite, and checkout starts with a ${TRIAL_DAYS}-day free trial.

Can I buy just one app?

Yes. Walkthru and Survey are each $12 a month or $120 a year on their own. Plot is free while it is in early access. You can add the others later, or move to the suite.

What does the suite include?

All three apps under one Kal Voe account, 50GB per user of storage, and hand-offs from Walkthru to Plot to Survey.

How much does the suite save?

Each app is $12 a month on its own, so all three would be $36. The suite is $24 per user a month, a saving of $12, or $240 per user a year against $360.

Is there a free plan?

Yes, in every app. Use it with no sign-up first, then make a free account. Survey’s free account has one project, five documents and every markup tool. No card needed.

Is there a yearly plan?

Yes. Every paid plan can be billed yearly, which gives you two months free: $120 a year for one app, or $240 per user a year for the suite.

How do I buy for a larger team?

The suite is priced per user. For single sign-on, seat management and an audit log, ask us about Enterprise.
