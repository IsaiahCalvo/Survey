What Plot is for

Draw the plan your installers build from.

Who it’s for

Designers, engineers and project managers who produce shop drawings for low-voltage and building systems work.

What you do in it

Lay out devices and cable runs on each floor, build risers, and keep the schedules in step with the drawing.

What comes out

An issued PDF set with title blocks, plus device schedules and a bill of materials in Excel.

Features

What you can do in Plot

01

Place devices, tagged for you

Drop devices on the floor plan and Plot numbers them as you go, so tags are never doubled or skipped.

  • Devices by type
  • Tags like CAM-01, numbered for you
  • Organized by floor
02

Run the cabling

Draw each cable run back to its closet. Every run gets its own number, so the drawing and the schedule agree.

  • Cable runs on the plan
  • Numbered runs
  • Home runs to the IDF
03

Build the riser

Show how every floor connects in a riser diagram, built from the same devices you placed.

  • Riser diagrams
  • Floors and backbones
  • Devices per floor
04

Schedules and a bill of materials

The device schedule and the bill of materials come from the drawing. Export them to Excel for the office.

  • Device schedules
  • Bill of materials
  • Excel (.xlsx) export
05

Issue the set

Each sheet gets its title block. When it’s ready, issue the set as a PDF.

  • Title blocks
  • Issued PDF sets
  • Revisions

Questions about Plot

Can I start in Plot without a Walkthru walk?

Yes. Plot works on its own with any floor plan. In the Kal Voe Suite, you can also open a Walkthru walk in it.

What can I export?

Issued PDF sets with title blocks, and device schedules and a bill of materials as Excel spreadsheets.

Does it handle more than one floor?

Yes. Devices are organized by floor, and the riser diagram shows how the floors connect.

How much does it cost?

Plot is $25 a month on its own, or included in the Kal Voe Suite for $29 per user a month.
