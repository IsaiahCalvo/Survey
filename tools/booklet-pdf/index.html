Questions

How do I print it?

Print on both sides of the paper and choose "flip on short edge". Keep the sheets in order, fold the whole stack in half and staple along the fold.

Why were blank pages added?

Each folded sheet holds 4 pages, so a booklet needs a multiple of 4. Blank pages are added at the end to make it fit.

Can I do several files at once?

Yes. Choose or drop several files. Each one gets the same settings, and you save them all in one ZIP.

What paper size should I pick?

Letter or A4 makes a booklet half that size. Tabloid makes Letter-size pages and A3 makes A4-size pages. "Two pages wide" keeps your pages at full size.

How many pages can a booklet have?

Any number, but past about 64 pages the stack is hard to fold. Split long files into parts first.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The tool runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device. Nothing is sent to us or kept after you close the tab.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark on your file and no daily limit.

Is there a file size limit?

Only what your device can hold in memory. Most phones handle files of a few hundred MB; a computer handles more.
