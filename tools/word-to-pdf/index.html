Questions

Will it look exactly like Word?

Close for simple documents: headings, bold and italic text, lists, tables, links and pictures carry over. Text boxes, columns, shapes, charts and headers or footers don't, and the tool tells you when a file has them.

Can it open old .doc files?

Not yet. Open the file in Word, Pages or Google Docs and save it as .docx, then try again.

Can I do several files at once?

Yes. Choose or drop several files. Each one gets the same settings, and you save them all in one ZIP.

Why not upload it to a server like other sites?

Keeping the file on your device means nobody else ever sees it. For complex layouts, Word's own "Save as PDF" gives the most exact result.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The tool runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device. Nothing is sent to us or kept after you close the tab.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark on your file and no daily limit.
