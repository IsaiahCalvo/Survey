What changed from the previous mockup

The chrome above now matches the real app — same dark grey toolbar, same tab bar, same left and right rails, same status corner. The only piece being redesigned is the right edge of the top toolbar row, where today there's a red color square and the number "2". That entire piece becomes the context-aware strip.

Pen and highlighter show a plain solid color circle, plus stroke width. Eraser shows only diameter. Selecting a shape brings up its fill color (with the new ring-around-disc trigger) plus border style and any extras. Editing text inside a callout or text box swaps the row to bold/italic/underline/strike, a 3-by-3 alignment grid, font, size, and font color.

Active tool icons turn gold, no fill, no permanent outline — matching the locked design system. Tooltips are the snappy light-grey style. Color picker mirrors the entity picker from the template editor.
