# Marks at the page edge (2026-11-02)

Isaiah's report: a picked rectangle near the top of the page had its rotate
grabber cut off by the page edge. He asked us to see how Drawboard PDF handles
marks that go past the page and copy it.

## What Drawboard does

Checked live on pdf.drawboard.com (no account, the "Read me" sample, a plain
rectangle, Playwright):

| Action | Drawboard |
| --- | --- |
| Grabbers (resize, rotate, frame) near the edge | Always drawn past the page edge, over the gap and the next page, and grabbable there |
| Draw a shape and drag past the edge | The shape stops at the edge |
| Resize a side past the edge | The side stops at the edge |
| Move (drag) past the edge | The mark's centre stops at the edge, so up to half the mark can hang off |
| Arrow-key nudge past the edge | No stop at all: the mark can leave the page and vanish |
| Rotate near the edge | Free; corners may go past the edge |
| The part past the edge | Shown in full while the gesture runs, hidden (clipped) once you let go |

Drawboard draws its rotate grabber below the shape. Survey keeps its own
grabber on top (the selection quick bar rules depend on it).

## What Survey does now

- Grabbers are never clipped. The page clip moved from the root `<svg>` onto
  the marks (`data-page-clip`), and the page holding the selection paints
  above the other pages (`data-page-has-selection`, styles.css).
- Drawing a shape and resizing an upright mark stop at the page edge, as in
  Drawboard (`src/utils/pageEdgeRules.js`). A side that already sat past the
  edge is never pulled back in.
- While a mark turns, the part past the edge shows; it is clipped again on
  release.
- Kept Survey's own rules where Drawboard is worse: a drag keeps the whole
  selection on the page (Drawboard can hide half a mark), and arrow-key nudges
  stay on the page (Drawboard can push a mark off the page entirely).

Tests: `tests/pageEdgeRules.test.mjs`,
`tests/e2e-signed-in/page-edge-grabbers.spec.mjs`.
