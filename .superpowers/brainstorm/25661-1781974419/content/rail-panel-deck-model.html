Design Rule

Tool rail is persistent and can live left or right. User preference decides hand side.

Panel deck is one shared drawer, not stacked sheets. Survey, Regions, Search, Pages, and Export can be open as modules inside it.

When drawing or hit-testing, deck collapses to chips so the PDF gets maximum touch area.

Survey + Regions together means both chips are active in the same deck, with one expanded and the other available above/below.
