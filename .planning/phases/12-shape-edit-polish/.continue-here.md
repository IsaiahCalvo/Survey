New spec: keep distance from SHAPE constant, computed at the worst-case orientation (handle horizontal, pill width facing shape). How to apply: `EXTENSION = handleHalfMax + gap + pillHalfMax` is a compile-time constant; `pillCenter = handleCenter + unit * EXTENSION` makes the pill orbit the shape at fixed radius.
