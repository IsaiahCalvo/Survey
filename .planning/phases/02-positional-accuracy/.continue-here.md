Architecture of current scroll correction:

1. Wheel handler (App.jsx ~11087): Before first zoomTo of a gesture, stores pre-zoom scroll + startScale in `zoomScrollCorrectionRef`. Calls `zoomTo(nextZoom)` without scroll correction.

2. handleSyncfusionZoomChange (App.jsx ~11666): On each zoomChange event, computes cumulative sf = nextScale / startScale. Sets scroll = startScroll * sf + cursorViewportY * (sf - 1). Also queues a microtask to re-apply the same correction (backup for async Syncfusion adjustments).

3. Settle timer (App.jsx ~11756): Clears `zoomScrollCorrectionRef` when zoom bridge deactivates (200ms after last zoom event).

- `syncfusionWheelZoomViewportAnchorRef` — cursor {x, y} relative to container viewport (no scroll)
